# Interaction Principles

## Philosophy

Interactions should feel calm.

Never playful for the sake of being playful.

---

# Hover

Subtle elevation.

No exaggerated scaling.

---

# Buttons

Soft transitions.

200ms duration.

---

# Cards

Lift

2–4px

Small shadow increase.

---

# Gallery

Fade

No abrupt appearance.

---

# Upload

Progress bar.

Estimated time.

Success animation.

---

# Errors

Friendly language.

Explain the issue.

Provide next action.

Never show raw technical errors.

---

# Loading

Skeleton loaders.

Avoid spinners when possible.
