# Product Goals

## Purpose

Define what success looks like for Project GeeNi.

---

# Primary Goal

Create a premium digital wedding experience that helps guests before, during, and after the wedding while preserving memories for years to come.

---

# Business Goals

- Replace printed invitations with a rich digital experience.
- Reduce guest confusion about schedules and venues.
- Collect high-quality guest photos and videos.
- Build a reusable platform for future celebrations.

---

# Guest Goals

Guests should be able to:

- Find event timings in less than 30 seconds.
- Navigate to any venue in one tap.
- Upload photos in under one minute.
- View galleries without needing technical knowledge.

---

# Bride & Groom Goals

Govind and Nidhi should be able to:

- Receive every guest memory.
- Download albums.
- Moderate uploads.
- Track RSVPs.
- View analytics.
- Revisit memories years later.

---

# Engineering Goals

- Lighthouse score >95
- Mobile-first
- Responsive
- WCAG AA
- Fast image delivery
- Zero downtime during wedding events

---

# Long-Term Goal

Project GeeNi should remain useful even after the wedding as a digital memory archive.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

- Guests require no explanation to use the website.
- Every major feature is mobile-friendly.
- Core pages load in under 2 seconds.
