# User Personas

## Persona 1 — Grandparent

### Goal

Find event details quickly.

### Needs

- Large buttons
- Clear text
- Venue maps
- Schedule

### Frustrations

- Complex menus
- Small fonts
- Too many animations

---

## Persona 2 — Friend

### Goal

Capture and upload memories.

### Needs

- Fast uploads
- Gallery
- QR code access

### Frustrations

- Slow websites
- Login barriers

---

## Persona 3 — Family Member

### Goal

Coordinate travel and attendance.

### Needs

- Accommodation
- Parking
- Event timings

---

## Persona 4 — Photographer

### Goal

Upload professional albums efficiently.

### Needs

- Bulk uploads
- Album management
- Metadata support

---

## Persona 5 — Bride & Groom

### Goal

Preserve memories and monitor the event.

### Needs

- Dashboard
- Analytics
- Moderation
- Downloads
- AI features

---

# Design Principle

Every feature must solve a real problem for at least one persona.
