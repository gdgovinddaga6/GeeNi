# Product Philosophy

## Purpose

Define the principles that guide every design and engineering decision.

---

# Philosophy

We are not building a wedding website.

We are building a digital experience.

---

# Principle 1

Emotion before information.

Guests should feel welcomed before they start reading.

---

# Principle 2

Luxury through simplicity.

Remove unnecessary UI.

Whitespace is intentional.

---

# Principle 3

Photography is the product.

The interface should disappear behind the memories.

---

# Principle 4

Mobile is the default platform.

Every feature must work beautifully on a phone.

---

# Principle 5

Accessibility is elegance.

A guest should never struggle to complete a task.

---

# Principle 6

Performance is a feature.

Beautiful websites that load slowly are not premium.

---

# Principle 7

Timeless over trendy.

Avoid design decisions that will look outdated in five years.

---

# Decision Filter

Before adding a feature, ask:

1. Does it improve the guest experience?
2. Does it reduce confusion?
3. Does it preserve memories?
4. Can older guests use it easily?

If the answer is "No" to all four, reconsider the feature.
