# Glossary

## Guest

Anyone attending one or more wedding events.

---

## Couple

Govind Daga and Nidhi Bang.

---

## Event

A scheduled wedding function such as Haldi, Sangeet, Reception, or Wedding Ceremony.

---

## Memory

A guest-uploaded photo or video.

---

## Gallery

A curated collection of memories.

---

## Upload Session

A single guest upload transaction.

---

## Time Capsule

Messages and memories unlocked on a future anniversary.

---

## Celebration Compass

The homepage component that dynamically shows guests what they should do next.

---

## Admin

Authorized users who manage content and moderation.
